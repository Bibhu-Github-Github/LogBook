Point 1: Access Control & Issue Raising
Only members of the Global Risk team should have permissions to raise issues in Aurora given our operating model. All other users across the organization should be restricted to read-only access for existing issues.
Point 2: Queue Management & Resourcing
The issue queue is not actively managed. Low, medium, and select high-severity issues are neglected and put on hold due to a severe lack of resourcing and funding for producers relative to the volume of issues handed over. Consequently, remediation teams are forced to focus exclusively on high-priority items.
Point 3: End-to-End Resolution Accountability
No comments provided.
Point 4: System Consolidation & Duplication
Aurora must serve as the primary and sole tool for logging and managing Data Quality (DQ) issues and data gaps, eliminating current duplications where users are directed to BI support teams, Jira, or Clarity. Critical-severity issues are the sole exception and must be logged via Helios.
Point 5: Complex Aurora Inputs & System Instability
Aurora suffers from operational instability, ongoing unannounced feature updates, and poorly socialized guidance that hinder logging. Most fields should be made optional rather than mandatory—such as "business process impacted"—because raisers genuinely cannot supply these values at the point of entry, causing widespread data inaccuracy.
Point 6: Senior Visibility & Governance
A dedicated governance body (such as a Data Governance Board or working group) must be established to review issues against Key Control Indicators (KCIs). This oversight will ensure adequate senior visibility, drive traction, and keep all issues progressing simultaneously without stalling.
Point 7: Operational Pain Points (Bulk Upload & Regax Issue Locking)
The bulk upload functionality is unstable and overly restricted, preventing data managers from efficiently maintaining the ~130 active Aurora issues at scale. Furthermore, high-severity regax issues lock automatically, creating unnecessary friction when incorporating new root-cause details. Data leads require a single-click bulk unlock capability to manage and update these records efficiently.

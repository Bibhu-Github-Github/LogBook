Following the completion of discussions and alignment sought in the Business Analytics/Governance Forum, the product-wise recommendations and decisions outlined below have been finalised for implementation.
1. Mortgages
a. 'Commercial and Factory Mortgages' is to be reported under Retail (non-SME), as approximately 95% of the exposure is attributed to Individuals, with the remaining ~5% attributed to shell companies; this is considered consistent with regulatory guidelines. It was agreed that this be mapped under Mortgages in the BA Forum held on 27th August (Gross Balance: USD 322Mn).
b. 'Car Park' facilities are to be classified under the 'Mortgages' category. This was agreed in the BA Forum held on 27th August (Gross Balance: USD 56Mn).
2. Overdraft and Loans
a. Secured Overdraft is to be reclassified as 'RWL' (Wealth), rather than 'Others', as the majority (80%+) of this balance is comprised of Secured OD. This was agreed in the BA Forum held on 6th July (Gross Balance: USD 520Mn).
b. Revolving Loan is to be classified as 'Loans'. This was agreed in the BA Forum held on 6th July (Gross Balance: USD 54Mn).
c. Restructure exposures are to be classified under 'Others – Restructure' (Gross Balance: USD 41Mn).
d. Securities Loans and Other Secured/Unsecured Loans are not to be reclassified; reporting under 'Others' is to be continued (Gross Balance: USD 0.3Mn).
e. Taxi and PLB exposures are to be classified as 'Secured Loans – Taxi & PLB', to be treated under 'Others' (Gross Balance: USD 240Mn).
3. Wealth / Private Banking
a. Wealth flagging is to be distinguished between RWL and Private Banking (PB), or alternatively the Level 11 sub-product is to be displayed from AIR data. This was agreed in the BA Forum held on 6th July.
b. 'Private Banking' is to be treated as a segment separate from Retail in MIDAS. This is currently under review by HASE HK; implementation is to be proceeded with once the review is concluded and a response is confirmed (Gross Balance: USD 1,672Mn under 'PB Lending', comprised of Mortgages, Overdraft, Term/Revolving Loans, Unauthorized OD, Share Margin, and Investment Financing sub-products).
4. Finance Reporting Alignment
a. Current product mappings and adjustments reflected in Finance records (under 'Loans') are to be continued, in line with Saracen/TM1 reporting, for Commercial & Factory Mortgages and Car Park, notwithstanding the variance with the classification of these products under 'Mortgages' by Risk.
b. The resulting data quality gap between Gross receivables (as reported by Risk) and Net receivables (as reported by Finance), arising from these adjustments, is not to be remediated. The variance is to be documented and the current High Severity DQ issue is to be closed; a separate Aurora issue is to be raised with 'Low' Severity for continued tracking.
c. It is noted that Wealth is treated as a single category by Finance (TM1), whereas a clearer separation between RWL and PB products is required by Risk; this is to be addressed as part of the Wealth/Private Banking reclassification described at Point 3 above.
